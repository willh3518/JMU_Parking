# Privacy Policy for Campus Park

**Last updated:** July 13, 2026

Campus Park ("the app") helps you find nearby parking. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

## Location

The app requests access to your device's location, in the foreground only, while you are using the app. This is used to:

- Show your current position on the parking map
- Calculate the nearest parking recommendations
- Provide walking/driving directions to a selected parking spot

Your location is never collected in the background, stored, transmitted to a server, or shared with any third party. It is used only, in real time, on your own device, to power the features above.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Campus Park does not:

- Require or support user accounts or logins
- Collect names, emails, or other personal identifiers
- Use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
- Store your search history or filter preferences beyond your current session (they reset when you close the app)
- Sell or share any data with third parties

## Third-Party Data Sources

The app fetches public parking availability data from James Madison University's public parking API and a public downtown Harrisonburg parking data feed (ArcGIS). These are one-way, read-only requests for public information — no personal or location data is sent to these services as part of these requests.

## Children's Privacy

Campus Park does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is intended for general use in finding parking near James Madison University's campus.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
